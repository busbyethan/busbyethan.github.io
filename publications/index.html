The Sound of Persuasion: Using Generative AI to Identify Gender Gaps in Political Influence

Ethan C. Busby, Lisa P. Argyle, Parker Davis, Joshua R. Gubler, Bryce Hepner, Christopher F. Karpowitz, Alex Lyman, Justin Olcott, Jessica R. Preece, and David Wingate

Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (forthcoming)

Summary & methods

Using real-time conversations with AI agents, this study separates voice-based gender cues from persuasive rhetoric and the topic of discussion. In this experiment, male- and female-sounding voices did not differ directly in persuasiveness; topic and participant gender were associated with differences in persuasion. The findings identify specific gender-related dynamics rather than establishing that gender bias is absent from political conversation.

Methods: randomized experiment, real-time voice conversations, generative AI, gender and persuasion
